Behind The Scenes (BTS) on Set

Professional Journey

With 10 years of experience working across film and TV environments, I have spent a decade developing an intimate knowledge of modern film sets, technical protocols, and professional departmental mechanics. My path began on the floor as an extra/supporting artist, before actively progressing through hands-on technical deployments as a Camera Trainee, 2nd AC, and Grip. This comprehensive across-the-line experience gave me a deep understanding of lenses, composition, and physical movement prior to channeling my focus into the lighting department. This multi-department footprint is paired with 4 years working within technical rental house infrastructure (2021–2025), alongside serving as a specialized public-sector videographer for the NHS during the Pandemic (2020-2021).
